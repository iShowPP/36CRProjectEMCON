City council approves riverside flood barrier

After three years of debate, the council voted 7–2 on Tuesday to fund a two-kilometre flood barrier along the riverside highway, which closed twice last monsoon.

The barrier will be built in stages starting next spring. Residents near the eastern bank will be consulted on access points before construction begins.
