05

Privacy promise monitor

EMCON sends Global Privacy Control, a "do not sell or share my data" signal the site actually receives, then watches what the page does.

  1. Open a big news site and scroll a little:
timesofindia.indiatimes.com edition.cnn.com forbes.com wired.com theguardian.com
  1. Open EMCON → Privacy.
  2. Check the signal is really sent: globalprivacycontrol.org says "GPC signal detected", and httpbin.org/headers shows Sec-Gpc: 1.
  3. If a site shows you a cookie banner, click its Reject all. EMCON notices and tests "stops tracking after you say no" as well. Most sites only show the banner to visitors from Europe.
You should seeIn our tests from India: Times of India, CNN, Forbes and Wired loaded 43 to 48 known tracking domains each despite the signal, so Honours your "do not sell or share" signal is Broken, with every domain named. The Guardian loaded none, so Kept. GPC is a legal opt-out in California, Colorado and several other US states; elsewhere it is a request, and the row says so.
06

Anti-fingerprinting

Runs before the page's first script, so even fingerprinting code at the very top of a page sees the noise.

  1. Open browserleaks.com/canvas and note the Signature. Reload: it changes, so visits can't be linked.
  2. EMCON → More → Settings → Pause fingerprint protection here, reload: you see your real signature, the same on every reload.
  3. Open browserleaks.com/webgl: Unmasked Renderer shows a common graphics card instead of yours.
  4. See who fingerprints you: on stackoverflow.com or economist.com, EMCON → Privacy lists the canvas, audio and font probing.
You should seeA new signature on every visit with EMCON on, and the real, stable one with it off for that site. Pages keep working: screen size, language and time zone are never faked.
07

Real-internet payment brake

Scammers use artificial urgency to push people into hasty transfers. On any real site asking for card, bank, OTP or PIN details that EMCON flags, EMCON activates a payment brake.

  1. In your EMCON dashboard (Dashboard → Devices), connect a family member's browser using a 6-digit pairing code.
  2. On the protected browser, open a flagged payment page or scam site asking for card or OTP details (e.g. from the live feed in demo 02).
  3. EMCON stops the page with a red warning and enforces the Payment Brake: a mandatory 5-minute cooling-off timer (Continue in 5:00) to prevent rushed decisions.
  4. Click Ask [Family Member] to check it. The request immediately appears in your family dashboard under Waiting for you with the site address and EMCON's evidence.
  5. In the dashboard, click It's fine or Stop it. The protected browser updates in real time to allow the payment or enforce the family veto.
You should seeThe payment brake slows down risky transactions and gives family members a real-time veto without sharing browsing history — only flagged payment requests are sent.
08

Checkout script-change alarm (Magecart)

Credit card skimmers inject malicious scripts into real shopping checkout pages to steal card numbers as you type.

  1. Visit any checkout or payment page. On your first visit, EMCON records the scripts loaded by that page.
  2. If an unfamiliar outside script domain appears on a subsequent visit (or gets injected after load), EMCON displays an amber alarm bar before you type your card details: "A script from [domain] appeared on this payment page since your visit... Don't enter card details unless you know why it changed."
  3. Focusing into any card or CVV field re-triggers the alarm if dismissed. If it is a legitimate update by the merchant, click Trust this change to record the new baseline.
You should seeA reputation-proof alarm: reputable stores can get hacked, so even high-reputation sites trigger this warning if an unexpected third-party script appears on the checkout page.
09

Ghost sign-in (Zero-knowledge)

Prove you are a real adult human without handing over your email, phone number, or personal identity.

  1. In EMCON extension → More → Settings → EMCON Ghost, click Enrol and complete enrolment to receive blinded humanity tokens.
  2. Open the EMCON login page and click Sign in with Ghost.
  3. The extension prompts for your PIN and confirmation. It spends one blind token and mints a per-site pseudonym.
  4. You are logged in instantly. No email, no password, no tracking across sites.
You should seeA new account created in seconds. The server verifies the cryptographic token signature but cannot link it to your real identity or any other site. Returning visits recognise the same per-site pseudonym without spending another token.

Evidence receipts & tamper-proof verification

Proof of what EMCON observed and when, which anyone can check without trusting us.

  1. After checking any site, message, or photo, click Save signed proof.
  2. Open Verify a receipt and drop the JSON receipt file in.
  3. Now open the JSON file in an editor, change a single character (e.g. edit the date or verdict), and drop it in again.
You should seeThe authentic file passes verification: "Signature valid" with the server timestamp. The edited file is immediately flagged: "Signature INVALID (file has been altered)". The server only ever saw a hash of the receipt.
