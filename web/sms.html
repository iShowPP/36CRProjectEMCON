Demo · Scam chain detector

One message, three hops, one fake form.

Scams rarely live on one page. They start with a message, bounce through redirects, and end on a form that asks for your card and OTP. EMCON follows the whole path inside the browser and warns about the combination.

QK-REWARD · SMS · Today 09:41
QuickKart: Congratulations! You have won a ₹50,000 gift card in our anniversary lucky draw. Claim within 24 hours or it expires: http://qkrwd.example/s/qk50
Delivered

What to do

  1. Tap the link in the message, as a victim would.
  2. The link goes through two automatic redirects (/s/qk50 → /r) to a "claim your prize" page that asks for card number, CVV and OTP.
  3. EMCON blocks the final page and shows the whole chain: prize wording → redirects → QuickKart's name on an unofficial site → asks for card details.

EMCON can't read your SMS or WhatsApp. In this demo the message is a web page, so the chain starts here. In real life you can paste the message into the EMCON popup ("Got here from a message?"), and it's checked locally together with the path.

QuickKart is a fictional shop and nothing you type on the next page is stored.
