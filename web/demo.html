Catches scams

Phishing and look-alike sites

Blocks copies of real login pages, look-alike addresses (paypa1-secure.com), brand-new domains and sites in the live phishing feed or reported by people you trust.

Scam chain detector

Follows the path to a page: the message, the short link, the redirects, the fake site, the form. Prize or threat wording plus card or OTP fields becomes one explained warning.

Prize SMS demo
  • Never reads your messages: you can paste one; it stays on your computer.
  • No false alarms on checkouts: redirects plus card fields alone never trigger it.

Honey-Vault payments

Scam brakes inside the bank: a risk-scaled cooling-off, a scam-script check-in, family guardians who co-sign or veto, a duress PIN, and settings that only loosen slowly.

Explains itself

Evidence receipts

Every check is shown as Warning, OK, Couldn't check or Not needed. Save a receipt signed with your EMCON key and timestamped by the server, which sees only a hash of it.

Verify a receipt
  • Honest limits: a receipt proves what EMCON observed and when, not that a site is guilty.

Privacy promise monitor

Names the companies tracking you on each page, sends "do not sell or share" (Global Privacy Control), and checks whether tracking stops after you say no.

Photo labels
