EMCON · hackathon build

Evidence, not guesses.

A browser extension that checks pages, redirect chains, images and payments, and shows you exactly why it reached each conclusion: signed reports, public registries, hashes and time-locks. No AI scores that a scammer can learn to fool.

Set up in two minutes
  1. In Chrome, Edge or Brave open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the emcon-extension folder.
  2. On the welcome page, set the server to .
  3. Pin EMCON to the toolbar.
Catches scams
01

Phishing and look-alike sites

Blocks copies of real login pages, look-alike addresses (paypa1-secure.com), brand-new domains and sites in the live phishing feed or reported by people you trust.

Fake bank loginReal bank login
  • Fake reviews: only reports reachable from your own trust roots count, capped per branch.
  • Privacy: lookups send a 5-character hash prefix, never the address.
02

Scam chain detector

Follows the path to a page: link clicks, redirects, shorteners, tabs opened from other tabs. Combines prize or threat wording, the hops and a request for card details into one explained warning.

Prize SMS demo
  • No mind-reading: EMCON never reads your messages. You can paste one; it stays local.
  • No false alarms on checkouts: redirects plus card fields alone never trigger it.
03

Honey-Vault payments

Scam brakes inside the bank: risk-scaled cooling-off, a scam-script check-in, guardians who co-sign or veto, a duress PIN, and settings that can only be loosened slowly.

Kestrel Bank
  • Coached victims: the delay grows with risk and red-flag answers go to guardians.
  • Thieves with your PIN: loosening a protection takes 24 hours and alerts guardians.
Explains itself
04

Evidence receipts

Every check is a row marked found, clear, unknown or not applicable. Export a receipt signed with your EMCON key and timestamped by the server, which sees only a hash of it.

Verify a receipt
  • Honest limits: a receipt proves what EMCON observed and when, not that a site is guilty.
05

Privacy promise monitor

Records which third parties a page contacts and which sensitive features it uses, then checks the site's declared promise and the legal baseline: tracking stops when you say no.

Newspaper demo
  • Observed, not inferred: every claim lists the domains and features your browser actually saw.
06

Media provenance

Asks "who published this, and has it changed?" instead of "is this AI?". Verified, altered, recovered after stripping, or unknown signer. Unsigned images get no label.

Provenance studioFeed
  • Stripped metadata: a visual fingerprint finds the signed original, matched on your machine.
Protects your identity
07

Ghost sign-in

Sign up as a verified adult with no email, name or cross-site ID: blind-signed tokens and a different key on every site.

Anti-fingerprinting

Stable per-site noise and bucketed hardware values, so trackers can't follow your device across sites and sites don't break.
