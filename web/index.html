How it works

It follows the whole scam, not just one link.

Scams start with a message, hide behind redirects and end on a fake form. EMCON watches the full path, then explains what it found.

A message arrives

"Your account will be blocked today. Update KYC: bit.ly/…". Paste it into EMCON, or just tap the link.

EMCON follows the trail

Short links, redirects, look-alike addresses, brand-new domains and live scam reports, checked before you type anything.

You see the proof

A red "Back to safety" page with every reason in plain words, and a signed receipt you can show your bank or the police.

Questions

Good questions to ask a security product.

Does EMCON read my messages or browsing history?

No. Messages you paste stay on your computer; only the link is sent to be followed. Phishing lookups send a 5-character hash prefix, not the address. Family dashboards show counts, never sites.

What is the payment brake and how does it protect my family?

When you or a connected family member visits a payment page asking for card, OTP, PIN or bank details that EMCON flags, EMCON enforces a mandatory 5-minute cooling-off period to break scam urgency. The page also offers "Ask <your name> to check it": the request appears on your dashboard within seconds with EMCON's evidence, and you approve it or stop the payment.

How does the checkout script skimmer (Magecart) alarm work?

On your first visit to a payment page (card fields in the page, or a Stripe, Braintree, Adyen or Razorpay card frame), EMCON records every script it loads. If a script from a new outside domain shows up on a later visit, EMCON warns you before you type card details: that is how "Magecart" skimmers steal cards from genuine shops. New scripts from the shop itself or from known ad and analytics services are noted, not alarmed.

How is this different from AI scam detectors?

AI detectors give a score you have to trust. EMCON shows every check: who reported a site, which brand it copies, which redirects you passed through. You can verify each one yourself.

How does EMCON check photos?

Photos from cameras and apps that support Content Credentials (C2PA), such as Truepic, Leica, Nikon and Adobe, carry a signed record. EMCON checks the signature, every recorded step and the image hash, and whether the signer's certificate chains to the official C2PA trust list. Publishers can also prove they own their website (a file at /.well-known/emcon-publisher.txt or a DNS TXT record) and sign photos in the dashboard. When a site strips the credentials, EMCON can still recognise the photo by its visual fingerprint. Photos without credentials get no label: missing provenance proves nothing.

What if EMCON gets it wrong?

Every warning lists its reasons, so a mistake is easy to spot. You can continue anyway, and vouch for a site you trust; your vouch counts for people who trust you.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key you saved at sign-up. Nobody at EMCON can reset your account, so nobody can be tricked into handing it over.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac and Linux. Mobile protection is on the roadmap.
