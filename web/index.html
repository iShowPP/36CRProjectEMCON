How it works

It follows the whole scam, not just one link.

Scams start with a message, hide behind redirects and end on a fake form. EMCON watches the full path, then explains what it found.

A message arrives

"Your account will be blocked today. Update KYC: bit.ly/…". Paste it into EMCON, or just tap the link.

EMCON follows the trail

Short links, redirects, look-alike addresses, brand-new domains and live scam reports, checked before you type anything.

You see the proof

A red "Back to safety" page with every reason in plain words, and a signed receipt you can show your bank or the police.

Questions

Good questions to ask a security product.

Does EMCON read my messages or browsing history?

No. Messages you paste stay on your computer; only the link is sent to be followed. Phishing lookups send a 5-character hash prefix, not the address. Family dashboards show counts, never sites.

How is this different from AI scam detectors?

AI detectors give a score you have to trust. EMCON shows every check: who reported a site, which brand it copies, which redirects you passed through. You can verify each one yourself.

What if EMCON gets it wrong?

Every warning lists its reasons, so a mistake is easy to spot. You can continue anyway, and vouch for a site you trust; your vouch counts for people who trust you.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key you saved at sign-up. Nobody at EMCON can reset your account, so nobody can be tricked into handing it over.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac and Linux. Mobile protection is on the roadmap.
