Privacy policy

Draft for the hackathon build. Have a lawyer review it before EMCON takes paying customers.

EMCON is a privacy product, so this policy is short: we collect as little as possible, and most of EMCON never talks to us at all.

What runs only on your computer

  • Anti-fingerprinting, photo labels, the privacy monitor and the scam-chain detector run inside your browser.
  • Messages you paste into EMCON stay on your computer. Only the link inside is sent to be followed.
  • Your EMCON signing keys never leave your browser.

What the EMCON server sees

WhenWhat we receiveWhat we keep
Phishing lookupsThe first 5 characters of a hash of the site's domainNothing
Checking a linkThe link you asked to checkNothing (rate-limit counters only)
Saving a receiptA hash of the receipt, to timestamp itNothing
Your accountName, email, password (hashed with scrypt)Until you delete your account
Connected browsersWeekly counts: pages checked, scams blocked, warningsThe latest week; never the sites visited
Sign-insTime, method and browser typeYour last 30 sign-ins, for your security

What we never do

  • Sell or share your data, or use it for advertising.
  • Read your messages, email or browsing history.
  • Show a family member which sites another family member visits.
  • Load third-party trackers or fonts on our pages.

Your rights

You can download all your account data or delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting is immediate. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR you can also ask us what we hold about you and have it corrected.

Payments

No payment provider is connected yet. When one is, card details will go to that provider directly and never touch EMCON's servers.
