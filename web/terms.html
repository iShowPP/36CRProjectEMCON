Terms of service

Draft for the hackathon build. Have a lawyer review it before EMCON takes paying customers.

The short version: use EMCON to protect yourself and people who agree to it. EMCON helps you spot scams, but it can't promise to catch every one.

1. Using EMCON

  • You need to be 13 or older, or have a parent's permission.
  • Only connect a browser to your account if its user knows and agrees. Family protection is for people who want it, not for monitoring someone in secret.
  • Don't use the link-check API to scan sites you aren't allowed to, or to overload them.

2. What EMCON does and doesn't promise

EMCON shows evidence: scam reports, look-alike addresses, redirects and more. A missing warning doesn't mean a site is safe, and a warning isn't a legal finding that a site is a scam. Always use your own judgement before sharing passwords, OTPs or money.

3. Your account

Keep your password and recovery key safe; we can't reset your account for you. You can delete your account at any time.

4. Plans and billing

The Free plan is free. Paid plans renew monthly or yearly and can be cancelled at any time, keeping access until the end of the period. During this hackathon build, paid plans run in demo mode and nothing is charged.

5. API

API keys are personal to your account. Keep them secret, respect the daily limits of your plan, and don't resell raw results as your own blocklist.

6. Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we'll tell you on the dashboard before the change takes effect.

7. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India.
