Installed

EMCON is on

EMCON checks the pages, images and payment flows you run into, and shows you its evidence. It never gives you a black-box score.

First: pin EMCON to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to EMCON. The icon shows ! on dangerous pages, ? where you should be careful, and ✓ on sites your network trusts.

Anti-fingerprinting active now

Sites can't follow you across the web using your device's canvas, graphics card or audio fingerprint.

Image provenance active now

Photos signed by trusted publishers are labelled Verified; edited copies are labelled Altered. Unsigned images get no label: missing provenance doesn't mean an image is fake.

Privacy promise monitor active now

Records which third parties a page contacts and which sensitive features it uses, then checks whether tracking stops after you say no.

Scam and phishing checks needs server

Blocks fake login pages, look-alike addresses and live phishing sites, and follows the chain of redirects that led you there.

Ghost sign-in needs setup

Sign up on sites that accept EMCON as a verified adult without sharing your email, phone or name.

Evidence receipts active now

Export a signed, timestamped receipt of any check to share with a friend, teacher or security team.

EMCON server

Ghost, scam reports, the live phishing feed and receipt timestamps come from an EMCON server. The other checks run entirely in your browser.
