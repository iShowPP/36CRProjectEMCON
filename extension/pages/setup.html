EMCON · Ghost sign-in

Set up Ghost

You verify yourself once with an issuer. In return you get a batch of blind tokens: the issuer signs them without seeing them, so it can never tell where you spend them.

Step 1

Lock Ghost to you

Every sign-in needs your fingerprint, face, security key or PIN, so malware or a borrowed laptop can't quietly use your identities.

No passkey? Use a local PIN instead
Step 2

One-time verification (demo issuer)

In production this is an ID scan, a bank KYC check or a national eID. The demo issuer only needs your date of birth. It keeps the eligibility result ("18+: yes") and discards the date.
